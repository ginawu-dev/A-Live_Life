A-Live Life Privacy Policy ♡

Last updated: October 5, 2026

A-Live Life helps you record and reflect on your life in a private, personal way. The app is free to use, and free users may see banner advertisements in some screens, but your private memories, journal and reflection content, photos, Future Me content, Life Compass content, and other personal life records are not provided to AdMob for advertising or advertising targeting.

In short: A-Live Life stores personal life data on your device and, when iCloud Sync is enabled, may also sync that data through your private Apple iCloud / CloudKit account. The app does not operate a developer-owned backend for storing personal life content. Free users may see limited banner ads on the Today and Calendar screens, while Plus is ad-free.

What personal life data may be stored

A-Live Life may contain highly personal user-created information, including:

  • Moment titles and journal or reflection text
  • Moods
  • Places entered or associated with moments
  • Photos
  • Future Me / Future Letter content
  • Life Item names and relationships
  • Special dates and birthdays
  • Countdown titles and dates
  • Core Values / Life Compass selections and reflections
  • Other personal memories and life-planning information

This information is used to provide the app's personal life-recording and reflection features. Depending on the feature and the user's settings, it may be stored locally on the user's device and/or in the user's private Apple iCloud / CloudKit storage when iCloud Sync is enabled. A-Live Life does not operate a developer-owned backend for storing this personal life content.

How your information is stored

Personal app data is stored on the user's device so the app can work offline and keep your life records private on your device. If iCloud Sync is enabled, applicable personal data may also sync through the user's private iCloud / CloudKit account associated with the user's Apple/iCloud services. This is not transferred to a developer-owned A-Live Life server for storage.

Advertising and AdMob

A-Live Life uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide banner advertising to eligible free users. Ads may appear on the Today and Calendar screens. Ads are not shown on personal writing, reflection, Future Me, Life Compass, Settings, or Plus purchase screens.

A-Live Life Plus provides the existing Plus features and an ad-free experience. Plus may be obtained through a monthly auto-renewable subscription, a yearly auto-renewable subscription, or a lifetime non-consumable one-time purchase. Lifetime Plus does not expire or automatically renew.

Google Mobile Ads may process advertising-related technical and usage information as part of providing, measuring, securing, and improving advertising services. This may include, as applicable, device identifiers, coarse location, product interaction or app interaction information, advertising data, diagnostic information, crash data, and performance data.

Important: A-Live Life does not send personal life content to AdMob for advertising or advertising targeting. Personal life content is not provided to Google for ad targeting or ad personalization. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Journal or reflection text
  • Moment content
  • Moods
  • Personal photos
  • Future Me / Future Letter content
  • Core Values or Life Compass answers and reflections
  • Life Item names or relationships
  • Birthdays or special dates
  • Countdown titles
  • Personal places entered into life records
  • SwiftData record identifiers
  • CloudKit record identifiers
  • Inferred emotional state
  • Inferred personal interests or categories derived from private life content

A-Live Life does not pass the user's private memories, journal text, life plans, photos, personal reflections, or other personal life records to Google for advertising or advertising targeting. Google does not receive or analyze this private content through A-Live Life's advertising features.

Consent and UMP

A-Live Life uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), where applicable, to present privacy and consent choices and to determine whether advertising requests may be made. Available choices may depend on the user's region and applicable privacy laws.

Photos

Photos are only used when a user chooses to add them to a moment or life item. A-Live Life does not obtain photos from the user's library without permission. Photos selected for A-Live Life remain part of the user's personal app data and are not provided to AdMob for advertising or advertising targeting.

Users can manage app photo permissions in their device settings.

Notifications and reminders

A-Live Life may use local notifications or reminders to help users keep track of dates, future plans, or important life events. This reminder information is not advertising data and is not provided to AdMob for advertising or advertising targeting.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed through Apple's StoreKit and the App Store. A-Live Life supports:

  • Monthly Plus subscription
  • Yearly Plus subscription
  • Lifetime Plus one-time non-consumable purchase

Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled according to Apple's subscription terms. Lifetime Plus is a one-time purchase and does not automatically renew. A-Live Life does not directly receive or store the user's full payment-card details.

Restore Purchases is supported.

Third-party services

A-Live Life uses or may rely on the following third-party services, as applicable to the app's functionality and user settings:

  • Apple / iCloud / CloudKit
  • Apple StoreKit / App Store
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

For more information, please review the official privacy information for these providers: Apple Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy, and Google AdMob privacy information.

Security

A-Live Life takes reasonable steps to help protect personal app data stored on the user's device or in the user's private iCloud / CloudKit account. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free, and users should keep their devices and Apple account credentials secure.

Children's privacy

A-Live Life is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. The app is intended for personal life-recording and reflection use by adults and older teens where permitted by law. A-Live Life does not knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that would violate applicable law.

Deleting your data

Users can delete moments, memories, and other personal content using the controls in the app. If iCloud Sync is enabled, deleting content may also sync across the user's devices associated with that iCloud account. A user can also remove A-Live Life and its local data by deleting the app from the device. iCloud data may be managed through Apple's iCloud services and device settings.

Changes to this policy

A-Live Life may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page, and the effective date will be updated to reflect the most recent revision.
