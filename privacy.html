A-Live Life Privacy Policy ♡

Last updated: 17 September 2026

A-Live Life helps you keep the moments, people, places and thoughts you do not want to lose. Your memories belong to you.

In short: A-Live Life stores the information you choose to save. Your data is stored on your device and may be synchronized through your private iCloud account when iCloud Sync is enabled.

Information you choose to save

A-Live Life may store information that you voluntarily enter or add, including:

  • Your name
  • Your optional birthday
  • Moments, writing, moods, categories and places
  • Photos you choose to attach
  • People, companions and other life items
  • Future letters
  • Countdowns
  • Core values and compass selections
  • Reflection content derived from your saved memories

How your information is stored

Your Life Calendar information is stored locally on your device so that the app can continue to work offline.

If iCloud Sync is enabled and available, your information may also be stored in A-Live Life's private iCloud container so it can be available across your Apple devices. Apple manages iCloud according to Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

Photos

A-Live Life only uses photos when you choose to add them to a moment or life item. The app does not obtain photos from your library without your permission.

Photos selected for A-Live Life are stored as part of your saved memories. You can manage photo permissions in your device settings.

Reflections and processing

Monthly Reflections, Story of My Year and Through the Years are calculated from your saved Life Calendar information.

Reflection processing does not use GPT or an external artificial intelligence service.

Subscriptions and payments

A-Live Life Plus subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes subscription payments through your Apple Account.

A-Live Life does not receive or store your payment card details. Subscription status is used to determine access to Plus features.

Information we do not sell

We do not sell your memories or personal information to third parties. A-Live Life does not use your memories for advertising.

Deleting your information

You can delete memories and other saved content using the controls provided in the app. If iCloud Sync is enabled, deletion may also synchronize across your devices.

You can also remove A-Live Life and its local data by deleting the app from your device. iCloud data may be managed through Apple's iCloud services and device settings.

Children's privacy

A-Live Life is not directed toward children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
