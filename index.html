A calmer way to keep life in view

A-Live Life is designed around the moments that matter most: a birthday, a new chapter, a small joy, a meaningful conversation, a turning point, or a memory you want to revisit. It does not require a traditional daily diary. Instead, it helps you gather the moments, milestones, and reflections that tell the story of your life over time.

Privacy and iCloud sync

Your memories are personal. A-Live Life stores the information you choose to save on your device, and if iCloud Sync is enabled it may sync through your private iCloud account. The App Store listing for A-Live Life states, “Data Not Collected.”

For a concise explanation of how personal data is handled, see the A-Live Life Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

What is a life calendar?

A life calendar is a way to capture the meaningful moments, milestones, and memories that shape a person's life over time, rather than only a daily record of tasks or events.

What is A-Live Life?

A-Live Life is a personal life calendar and memory journal for iPhone and iPad. It helps you save meaningful moments, revisit memories, write to your future self, and reflect on how your life changes over time.

How is A-Live Life different from a traditional diary or journal?

A-Live Life is designed around meaningful moments, memories, milestones, and reflection over time rather than requiring a daily diary. You can save what matters most and revisit it later through your life calendar.

Do I need to write something every day?

No. A-Live Life is designed to help you capture meaningful moments as they happen, without requiring a daily diary entry.

Can I save memories and photos?

Yes. You can save meaningful moments with details such as photos, moods, places, dates, and notes, and connect people to the memories you want to keep.

Can I write a letter to my future self?

Yes. A-Live Life includes a Future Me feature for writing letters to your future self so you can leave encouragement, reflection, or perspective for later.

Can I track future milestones or countdowns?

Yes. You can track upcoming milestones and countdowns so your life calendar helps you stay connected to what is coming next.

Can A-Live Life help with personal reflection?

Yes. Features such as My Compass, Monthly Reflections, Story of My Year, and Through the Years are designed to help you reflect on patterns, memories, and life change over time.

Does A-Live Life collect personal data?

According to the App Store listing, A-Live Life is marked as Data Not Collected. The app stores the information you choose to save on your device and may sync through your private iCloud account when iCloud Sync is enabled.

Is A-Live Life available for iPhone and iPad?

Yes. A-Live Life is designed for iPhone and iPad.
